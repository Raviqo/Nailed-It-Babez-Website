BOOKING POLICY

A few simple rules so every appointment runs smoothly.

01

R100 Deposit

A R100 deposit is required to secure your booking and is deducted from your final bill. Your appointment is confirmed once the deposit has been received.

02

24-Hour Notice

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. With enough notice, your deposit may be moved once to a new appointment date.

03

Late Cancellations & No-Shows

Late cancellations and no-shows may result in the deposit being used as a cancellation charge. A new deposit may be required before booking again.

04

Late Arrivals

If you are more than 15 minutes late, the service may need to be shortened or rescheduled depending on the next booking.

05

No Children

Children are not permitted during appointments. The home studio contains professional tools and products, and uninterrupted working time is needed.

06

Please Come Alone

Please attend alone unless an additional guest has been arranged in advance.

07

Nail Health

Please tell us about any infection, open wound, allergic reaction or nail condition before your appointment. A service may be postponed if it is not safe to continue.

08

Repairs

If you experience lifting or a possible workmanship issue soon after your appointment, send a clear photo as soon as possible so it can be assessed.

09

Detailed Nail Art

Send detailed nail-art inspiration before your appointment so we can confirm the time required and whether there is an extra charge.

Genuine emergencies are considered individually. The full home address is shared privately after a booking has been confirmed.
